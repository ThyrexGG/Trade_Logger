## K. Event definitions (causal, §5)

- **M1**: the bar that *completes* a run of exactly 3 consecutive same-sign
  1-bar returns (fires once per streak, not on every bar within it).
- **V1**: the bar that first reaches 3 *consecutive* bars with ATR percentile
  rank ≤ 0.10 (duration-conditioned — distinct from Phase 76's single-bar
  compression test).
- **V2**: any bar whose trailing 4-bar realized-vol, ranked over the trailing
  200 bars, is > 0.66.
- **BR1**: the first bar closing beyond the prior 20-bar structural high/low
  (current bar excluded via `shift(1)`), THEN the first bar (within 20 bars)
  whose range touches back to that level — event fires at the retest bar.
- **BR2**: a breakout that, within 3 bars, never extends beyond its own
  extreme AND falls back through the broken level; **decision bar = breakout
  bar + 3** (strictly after the 3-bar classification window — no leakage of
  that window into the studied forward return).
- **S1**: every session-boundary bar; direction = sign of the 4-bar log-return
  strictly *before* the transition.

**What was available at event time t, and what was not:** every feature
(ATR, ATR-rank, realized-vol-rank, structural high/low, consecutive-bar
counters) is computed with `rolling(...).shift(1)` or an equivalent trailing
window that excludes bar t's own close where the definition requires it (e.g.
breakout levels). BR2's "failure" classification is the one case that uses
information *through* bar i+K to label the event — documented explicitly, and
the studied forward window starts at i+K, never earlier, so nothing from the
classification window leaks into the target. `test_no_lookahead_future_bars_do_not_change_past_events`
and `test_breakout_uses_prior_bars_only_not_current` assert this mechanically.

## L. Forward targets (§6)

- **Signed continuation** (M1, BR1, BR2, S1): `phase76_event_study.study_events`,
  unchanged — forward log-return over h∈{1,2,4,8} bars, divided by ATR at the
  event bar, signed by the event direction.
- **Range-expansion ratio** (V1): `(Σ true_range over next h bars) /
  (ATR_STABLE_i · h) − 1`, minus the same ratio's unconditional mean over the
  whole dev/OOS slice (§16 conditional-minus-baseline). `ATR_STABLE` is a
  trailing-200-bar mean ATR — see §S for why this is not the event-time ATR.
- **Regime-persistence probability** (V2): `P(future realized-vol percentile
  > 0.66 at i+h)`, minus the unconditional `P(bucket)` at the same horizon.

Both new (magnitude/probability) targets are explicitly baseline-centred so
the same POSITIVE/NEGATIVE/ZERO_CROSSING bootstrap verdict logic (built for
return-like data) is meaningful for them too.

**A methodologically important limitation, disclosed rather than hidden**:
V2's target (realized-vol-percentile persistence) is built on a rolling
realized-vol window. At very short horizons an overlapping rolling statistic
is mechanically autocorrelated with itself, independent of any real
phenomenon. This was addressed by (a) using a short (4-bar) realized-vol
window so the headline horizon (4 bars) sits at the boundary of full window
disjointness, and (b) the null-control check (§17/§AA): on synthetic
random-walk data, the *same* h=4/h=8 cells that are strongly positive on real
MT5 data are ZERO_CROSSING (z≈0) — i.e. the overlap alone does not produce a
false positive at the headline horizon. The h=1/h=2 cells (heavier overlap) do
show inflation on random data too and should be read with that in mind; the
headline (h=4) and h=8 results are the ones actually load-bearing for the
`ML_TARGET_READY` conclusion.
